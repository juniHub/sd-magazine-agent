🚀 Summer Micro-Adventures

Easy ways to create magical family memories without big budgets or time off work

💡 The Big Idea

Summer doesn't have to be big to be meaningful — it just has to be intentional. Kids don't measure summer by price tags. They remember popsicles that melt too fast, late-night backyard play, and the feeling that this time is special.

⛺

🌅

Breakfast Picnic

Blanket in the yard before work. 20 minutes shifts the day.

💦

Turns an ordinary Tuesday into something memorable.

🎨

Modern Dads

How they're redefining fatherhood
3×
Today's dads spend nearly three times as many hours with their children as fathers did in the 1960s

🌟 The Shift

Today's dads are embracing vulnerability, hands-on caregiving, and deeper emotional connections — modeling a more engaged version of fatherhood for the next generation.

"They're across everything — housework, kids, work, the lot. Compared to my dad's day, we've just had a slow, generation-by-generation change… being a dad just seems like an all-in."

— Joe Brumm, creator of Bluey

🏠 Redefining Gender Roles

The number of stay-at-home fathers has more than doubled over the past two decades as dual-income households rethink how responsibilities are divided.

🧠 Why It Matters for Kids

  • Fewer mental health challenges
  • Lower levels of aggression
  • Stronger relationships
  • A father's vocabulary with a 2-year-old helps predict language development at age 3!
📵 Screen-Free Ways to Connect
🎣

Get Outside

Go fishing, toss a ball, or take a walk to naturally spark conversation.

🔨

Build Something Together

A Home Depot workshop or model airplane — it's about the experience.

🎪

Play Their Way

From tea parties to LEGO, when parents enter a child's world, kids feel seen.

🏟️

Catch a Game

Talk about teamwork, effort, winning and losing — at any level.

🍩

Create a Simple Ritual

Saturday donuts, afterschool walks, or weekly "dad dates." Consistency beats complexity.

"Fatherhood has given me purpose. My identity now is being a good and present father."

— Deren Schneider, Rancho San Diego
